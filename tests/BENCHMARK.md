# Benchmark

Generated by `node tests/bench.js --write` — engine 4.0.0, 2026-10-25.

The posts below were written to cover the layouts and edge cases in the brief, alongside the engine. They are a
regression baseline, not a sample of real posts: a layout that isn't here can still trip it up. Add any post that
does to `tests/fixtures/corpus.js` and it is checked from then on.

Without labels, a card comes up unasked only for a heading, or a first or bold line in CAPS or Title Case: a sentence-case first line plus hashtags is just as often a tweet or LinkedIn draft. Those stories are still found when you click the toolbar icon.

## Misses

A short caption pasted on its own reads like a two-sentence headline (“He waited 40 years for this letter. Then
it finally arrived.”) or a headline with one line of story under it, so its first line is taken as the headline
and marked to check first: nothing runs on it until you confirm. 3.4 took any short single block as the headline
without asking.
